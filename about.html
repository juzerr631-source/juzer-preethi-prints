A Little About Me

Hi, I'm Preethi!

I'm a photographer, a writer, and someone who has always enjoyed creating things in my own little way. My interests have never stayed within one box. From photography and writing to art and building ideas from scratch, I find joy in the process of making something that feels personal to me.

Photography has become one of the ways I express myself. I love noticing little details, finding beauty in ordinary objects, observing the world around me, and capturing moments that catch my eye. Sometimes it's a quiet scene, sometimes a busy street, and sometimes something so simple that you might walk past it without a second glance.

The StoryTeller Frames is a new chapter in that journey, one where I get to share my photographs beyond my own gallery and give them a place in someone else's world. Building this with a friend makes it even more special, as we each bring our own way of seeing things to the table.

For me, this isn't just about selling photographs. It's about sharing a little of how I see the world and hoping that someone, somewhere, finds a photograph that feels like it was meant for them.

Welcome to my little corner of the world.
