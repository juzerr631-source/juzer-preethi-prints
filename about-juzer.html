A Little About Me

Hi, I’m Juzer!

I’m a photographer based in Pune, and I’m often drawn to things that are easy to walk past—a shadow crossing a building, a reflection in an unexpected place, or an ordinary street that feels different when the light changes.

Photography gives me a reason to slow down and look closer. I enjoy finding shapes in architecture, patterns in everyday surroundings, and small details that give a scene its character. Sometimes I go out with my camera; other times, the clouds outside my balcony are enough to keep me looking.

My photographs often lean towards deep shadows, muted colours, and black and white. I like images that leave a little room for the viewer’s imagination. Movies and their soundtracks are a big part of my world, and that love of atmosphere finds its way into how I photograph and edit.

The StoryTeller Frames is a chance to share those photographs beyond a screen. Building it with a friend means bringing two different perspectives together, while giving each of us space to stay true to our own eye.

I hope you find something here that makes you pause—something you’d enjoy looking at again, in your own space, in your own way.

Welcome to my side of the story.
