A little about me

Hi, I'm Juzer!

Photography, for me, started with a simple habit of noticing things.

I've always been drawn to the little details that are easy to walk past — light falling across a building, patterns and symmetry in architecture, a quiet street, changing skies, birds, shadows, reflections, or simply an ordinary moment that somehow feels worth remembering.

Over time, picking up a camera became my way of slowing down and looking at the world a little differently. I enjoy experimenting with different styles rather than limiting myself to one kind of photography. Some days I find myself chasing geometry and strong lines through the city, while on others, the camera ends up pointed at the moon, a bird outside my window, or something completely unexpected.

What I love most about photography is that something doesn't have to be extraordinary to make an interesting photograph. Sometimes it just needs to be noticed.

Made to Frames gives those photographs another life beyond a screen or a gallery — a chance for the moments Preethi and I capture to become part of someone else's space and story.

I'm still exploring, learning, and finding new ways to see things. And somewhere along the way, I hope to keep turning the ordinary into photographs worth putting on a wall.

— Juzer
